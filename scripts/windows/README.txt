Stratoscope for Windows

To install: double-click "install.cmd". It installs Stratoscope for you and starts it.
Then look at your taskbar, next to the clock: that is Stratoscope.

To remove it: Windows Settings > Apps > Stratoscope.

Even easier: the Setup on the download page ("Stratoscope-...-windows-setup.exe") does the same in a window that tells you what is happening.
